By bypassing the LLM and routing everything through t-functions (programs that perform specific processes), I have structurally eliminated the entry points for fabrication.

When you let an AI structure a script, there is always an inherent risk that unintended fabrications will creep in. You might think this can be solved by improving the precision of the prompts, but as long as you are using a probabilistic mechanism like generative AI, complete elimination is difficult.

Therefore, I added a new screen component dedicated to evidence in the center panel of the explainer. Instead of passing through structuring by Gemma (a type of lightweight LLM), it is a mechanism that directly extracts quotation lines from approved cards and performs keystroke playback (the effect of displaying characters one by one). By creating this "LLM-zero" state, I have structurally suppressed the entry points for fabrication specifically for these slides, and ensured reliability by displaying the card ID as a source stamp in the bottom right.

In other words, I have designed a structural direct-quotation route to guarantee "correctness."
