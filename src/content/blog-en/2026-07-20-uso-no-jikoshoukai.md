Companies say, "We can do anything," and applicants say, "I'll do anything." Both are erasing their own information to increase the probability of a match. The more information you strip from your self-introduction, the easier it becomes to get a job offer—which is why a mismatch becomes inevitable. It is a handshake between an empty box and another empty box.

Furthermore, "interview prep" is simply practice for this lie; the system itself teaches us how to misrepresent ourselves. Even though no one is a bad person, an equilibrium has been established where everyone gives a fake self-introduction.

A correct self-introduction isn't a matter of how you embellish it. It is a matter of the materials used. You cannot write it based on desires. You can only write it based on records.
