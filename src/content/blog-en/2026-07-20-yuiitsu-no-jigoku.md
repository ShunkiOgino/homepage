Giving up is a correct answer. Lower your desires to the floor and live light. No one can blame you for that.

Building is also a correct answer. Carry the equipment necessary to match your ambitions.

The only ones who are punished are those in the middle—those who keep their desires on the "I want to change" side, while their bodies remain in days where nothing is being built. They wait for a promised ladder. They buy lottery tickets. They say, "someday."

The difference between prayer and design is simply whether or not you are moving the variables that your own hands can control.

If you feel like you're working hard but not being rewarded, before looking at the amount of effort, first look at whether the place where you are striving is actually connected to the plumbing of what you want.

Every choice is permitted. The only things that are punished are the failure to choose, and the discrepancy.
