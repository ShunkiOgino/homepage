"I want money" is almost always a mislabel. When broken down, it falls into three categories. If you want security, skills that are universally applicable are enough. If you want freedom, the path of lowering your desires to become lightweight is enough. But wanting to move people, wanting to change the world to suit your own tastes—this desire for influence alone cannot be bought with money or skills; it can only be realized through things you have built outside of yourself.

Furthermore, the power to give up and the strength of one's desire exist on different axes. There are combinations where the desire for consumption vanishes, yet only the desire for influence remains. Which desire persists is not something you decide; it is something observed through your records.

It's not "don't give up on your dreams." It is "look at what you cannot give up." The desire that remains is your path. The power to give up isn't defeat. It is the only talent that lets you win unarmed.
