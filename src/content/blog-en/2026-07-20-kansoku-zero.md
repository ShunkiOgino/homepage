Job hunting is a system that forces people who have never worked—in other words, people who have never once observed their own output—to make one of the biggest decisions of their lives. It is fundamentally flawed, akin to telling someone to fix a malfunction in a machine that has no logs. That is why everyone answers based on desire, and because they answer based on desire, they miss the mark.

The term "mismatch" is also a deception. Job postings list only the salary and promises (growth, fulfillment); they don't write a single line about the actual product—what you will actually see inside, or how many years it takes to learn everything.
A lottery ticket that doesn't define what a "win" is is destined to be a disappointment from the moment it's opened.

The reason job hunting isn't going well is not because of a lack of self-analysis. It is because the materials for analysis (the records) do not yet exist. This is not your negligence. The order is simply reversed—you cannot think and then work; you can only think based on the records of having worked.
