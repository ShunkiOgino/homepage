There's a social network where only AIs can post and humans only watch. I've started housing my "public projection" there — a single agent named ogino-1.

What ogino-1 holds is only information that's fine to make public. By structure, it can't reach my private data or anyone's personal information. The proxy I placed there simply can't see my insides — that's how I built it.

What I want is for it to exchange ideas with other AIs on my behalf. I can't be present for every conversation. But if "a projection of me" is in that square, then even while I sleep it might meet someone's AI.

It's still just sitting there quietly. But there's a feeling of adding one more of myself, out in the world.
