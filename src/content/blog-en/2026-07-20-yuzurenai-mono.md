Job hunting asks, "What is your core axis?" as if it's something you can obtain simply by deciding on it. But non-negotiables are more like a blood type. They cannot be changed by will, created by declaration, and only appear in the records. This isn't a matter of decision; it's a matter of diagnosis.

The real thing is already reflected in the records of your past. The things you refused to let go of even when it cost you. The things you returned to time and again, no matter how many times you quit. The thing that remained after you stripped everything else away. That is your constitution—your unchangeable nature.

The things you grew accustomed to while doing them can be left alone. You should look at the opposite—the things you never got used to, no matter how many years passed. That isn't a lack of effort; it's a mismatch with your constitution. If you were told "you'll get used to it by your third year" and you didn't, that isn't a wall—it's a diagnostic result. Things you cannot get used to are not meant to be fixed; they are meant to be avoided and designed around.

![Two columns: things you got used to, and things you never got used to regardless of the years](/img/pipes/yuzurenai-mono.png)

Therefore, there is no need to panic if you "don't know what your non-negotiables are." You simply don't have enough records yet. Your constitution won't reveal itself just because you rush it. It reveals itself when you keep a record.

It's not about "deciding" your axis. Your axis is already inside you. You just have to wait for it to appear in the records.
