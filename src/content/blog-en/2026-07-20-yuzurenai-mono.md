Non-negotiables are like your blood type. They cannot be changed by will, created by declaration, and only reveal themselves through a record of your actions. The real ones are already reflected in the records of your past—the things you refused to let go of even at a loss. The things you returned to time and again, no matter how many times you quit. The thing that remained after everything else was stripped away.

That is why there is no need to panic if you "don't know what your non-negotiables are." You simply don't have enough records yet. Your constitution does not appear just because you rush it. It appears when you keep a record.

This is not a matter of decision, but a matter of diagnosis. It is not about "deciding on an axis"—the axis is already inside your body. You just have to wait for it to appear in the records.
