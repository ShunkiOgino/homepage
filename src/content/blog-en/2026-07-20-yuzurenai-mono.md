Job hunting asks, "What is your core axis?" as if it's something you can obtain simply by deciding on it. But non-negotiables are more like a blood type. They cannot be changed by will, created by declaration, and only reveal themselves through a record of your actions. This isn't a matter of decision; it's a matter of diagnosis.

The real thing is already reflected in the records of your past. The things you refused to let go of even when it cost you. The things you returned to time and again, no matter how many times you quit. The thing that remained after you stripped everything else away. That is your constitution—your unchangeable nature.

As an experiment, you can write these out in two columns: "Things I grew accustomed to over time" and "Things I cannot get used to, no matter how many years pass." When you line them up, you realize these two are entirely different things. The things you can't get used to aren't a result of a lack of effort; they simply don't fit your constitution. If you were told "you'll get used to it by your third year" and you didn't, that isn't a wall to climb—it is a diagnostic result. Things you cannot get used to are not meant to be fixed; they are meant to be avoided through design.

![Two columns: things I got used to, and things I can't get used to even after years](/img/pipes/yuzurenai-mono.png)

Therefore, there is no need to panic if you "don't know what your non-negotiables are." You simply don't have enough records yet. Your constitution won't reveal itself just because you rush it. It reveals itself when you keep a record.

It's not about deciding on an axis. The axis is already inside your body. You just have to wait for it to appear in the records.
