Self-analysis fails because it asks for desires. The answer to "what do I want to do" is always contaminated—by parents, by society, or by an advertisement seen moments before. On the other hand, "what did I stick with" is a fact that has already occurred; it cannot be rewritten by anyone.

The observational data isn't zero. The things you kept doing without being told. The things you realized you had stopped doing. Unlike a statement of purpose, a record of actions cannot be exaggerated.

The way to know yourself is not through introspection. It is through the interpretation of your own records.
