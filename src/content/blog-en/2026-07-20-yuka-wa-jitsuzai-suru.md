The fear surrounding job hunting is driven by the premise that "it's over if you fail." The inability to make a decision, the desperate clinging, the inability to leave a seat you hate—the root of all this is this single premise. And that premise is wrong.

Provided you have a healthy body and environment, you won't die if you fall in this country. A minimum standard of healthy and cultured living is guaranteed as a right; there is a floor beneath you. From benefits when you lose your job to housing support, reductions in insurance premiums and pensions, and finally public assistance—the systems are tiered, and legally, there is nothing below that. If the floor allows for survival, then no matter which path you choose, the downside is finite.

![The tiers of the system. The floor is at the very bottom.](/img/pipes/yuka-wa-jitsuzai-suru.png)

The important thing is not to believe this, but to research it. No one can give you a guarantee that "it'll be okay," and the amounts and conditions vary depending on where you live and your assets. That is exactly why you should search using the name of your own municipality. In ten minutes, your "floor" becomes a concrete number. The "end" in "it's over if I fail" does not exist within the system. It only exists in the imaginations of people who haven't looked into it.

It is common to harbor only the fear of the "end" without ever once researching what actually happens when your savings run out. The reason this isn't taught in school or at work is not because it's being hidden, but because there is no one assigned to teach it. Promises of a ladder are only sold at high prices to those who cannot see the floor. From the night you research it, your choices become a step lighter.

And then, the proviso becomes the primary article for everything. The only capital for a relaxed way of living, the seed money for building a path outward, and the strength to do so—the foundation they all sit upon is your single physical body. No matter which path a person chooses, the primary article is the same: at the very least, protect your health.
