The fear surrounding job hunting is driven by the premise that "it's over if you fail." That premise is wrong. Provided you have a healthy body and a stable environment, you won't die even if you fall in this country—a minimum standard of healthy and cultured living is guaranteed as a right, providing a floor beneath you. If the floor allows for survival, then no matter which path you choose, the downside is finite.

The "end" in "it's over if I fail" does not exist within the system. It exists only in the imaginations of people who haven't looked into it. Since amounts and conditions vary depending on where you live, you should just search using the name of your own municipality. From the night you research it, your choices become a bit lighter. The promise of a ladder sells at a high price only to those who cannot see the floor.

And that proviso becomes the primary article for everything. The sole capital for a relaxed way of living, the seed money for building a path outward, and the strength to do so—the foundation they all sit upon is your own body. No matter which path a person chooses, the first article is the same: at the very least, protect your health.
